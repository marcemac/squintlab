## [0.0.1] - 2026-11-03

Early pre-alpha release. This is a scaffold: it has no analyses yet, and they arrive in later versions.

### Added

- Package skeleton: `squintlab` installs and imports, and reports its version.
- Continuous integration on three systems (Linux, macOS and Windows) with Python 3.11, 3.12 and 3.13.
- Benchmark manifest and a fetch tool for downloading the public-domain benchmark images. Test images are never committed.
